## f11 [factual] What is the difference between the in-person and online programs?

The In-Person and Online Master's in Applied Data Science programs differ primarily in their delivery format and scheduling:

- **Delivery Format**: The In-Person program requires students to attend classes on campus, while the Online program allows students to participate remotely.
